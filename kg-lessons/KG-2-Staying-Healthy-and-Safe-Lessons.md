# KG-2 Lessons — Staying Healthy and Safe (December, 4 weeks)

**Level:** Kindergarten II (KG-2, ≈ ages 5–6) — Intermediate → Advanced
**Theme:** Staying Healthy and Safe · **Month:** December
**Source:** *Liberia National ECD Curriculum: Teacher Planner, 3- to 6-Year-Olds —
Staying Healthy and Safe*, Ministry of Education / Bureau of Early Childhood
Education, 2017 (`909924202-2-Staying-Healthy.pdf`). Page numbers below
(“Planner p. N”) are the printed planner pages.

**Sections:** I. Keeping My Body Healthy (Weeks 1–2) · II. Caring for Our
Community (Weeks 3–4).

## How to teach KG-2 from these plans

- **Children explain, not just repeat.** After you model once, ask *why* and
  *how*: “Why do we rinse ALL the soap off? What happens if germs stay?”
- **Charts, graphs and stories every week.** KG-2 children count findings,
  stick picture graphs, dictate or write stories, and make signs and labels.
- **Leaders and jobs.** Rotate daily leaders (song leader, line leader,
  materials helper, handwashing checker) — responsibility is part of the
  curriculum.
- **Rule-based games.** Traffic-light freeze, sorting races and guessing games
  with real rules and turns.
- **Assess by watching + work samples**, using the “👀 Look for” checkpoints
  each Friday. Keep one folder per child (drawings, dictated stories, charts).
  No written tests.

Daily routine: Morning Meeting 9:00–9:15 · Work Time + Small Groups 9:15–11:00
· Clean-Up 11:00–11:15 · Outdoor 11:15–12:00 · Wash-Up/Lunch 12:00–12:45 ·
Rest 12:45–14:15 · Story Time 14:15–14:30 · Closing 14:30–15:00 · Parents join
in all day (volunteer, sing, learn activities for home; accompany walks).

# SECTION I — KEEPING MY BODY HEALTHY (Weeks 1–2)

**Big idea (Planner p. 1):** Health is being well in body and mind, getting
along with others, and eating healthy food. KG-2 children learn the *reasons*
behind hygiene habits, practice them independently, investigate germs, teeth,
exercise and poisons, and record what they find. **The teacher models every
habit** — and KG-2 children begin to model habits for younger ones.

## Master materials — Section II (collect once, reuse all fortnight)

Soap, plastic bowl, water, pouring cups (⚠️ no running water: one cup to wet,
one to rinse; own named towel per child — never share wash water), toothbrush
+ paste, comb/brush, nail brush, sponge, poster of children washing (Planner
p. 4); egg boxes + sticks + white paint/chalk, sweet wrappers, sugar packet,
fizzy-drink bottle (p. 6); straws (pawpaw stems), whistles, tissues, bins,
face diagram if available (p. 11); sorting sets — food vs. non-food
(non-poisonous), pictures of poisonous/non-poisonous items, paper, markers,
tape (pp. 9, 13); paper, pencils, crayons; doctor kit + bandages (drama
hospital corner); body/skeleton poster; athlete pictures (male + female);
chart paper + stickers for graphs.

## WEEK 1 — Keeping My Body Healthy, part 1

**Weekly goals:** Children perform the 7-step handwash independently and can
teach it; explain in own words why we wash, brush, exercise and rest; sort and
**chart** safe/unsafe mouth items; invent and write/dictate a germ story.

### Day 1 (Monday) — Handwashing, expert level (pp. 4–5, 10)

- **Morning Meeting — Keeping Clean and Healthy:** Welcome, greetings, week’s
  topic. Children (not teacher) hold up each cleaning item, name it and
  explain its use; class corrects and adds. Discuss: *Why do we wash? When
  exactly? How do clean people feel — and why?* Appoint the week’s
  **handwashing checkers** (2 children with a picture checklist).
- **Small Group — Handwashing + germ stories:** (1) One child demonstrates all
  7 steps while the group chants (wet → soap/bubbles → palms → backs →
  between fingers → all fingers → rinse → dry). Checkers tick steps. (2) Draw
  instruction cards for the washing corner (traced hands + bubbles + step
  numbers). (3) **Advanced:** draw a germ, invent its story (“Gary the Germ
  travels from dirty hands to the rice…”), dictate or write it; teacher binds
  a class *Germ Book*.
- *Centers:* Art — germ gallery. Drama — doll clinic washes hands before
  “cooking.” Literacy — sequence cards (1–7) to order. Manipulative — food/
  dish-washing extension (Planner p. 5).
- *Outdoor:* **Water Play** — pouring relays, washing toy dishes “for the
  restaurant.”
- *Story Time:* read two Germ Book stories aloud; class suggests safer
  endings.
- *Closing:* “Who can teach handwashing to KG-1 tomorrow?” (volunteers!)
- *Home link:* child teaches the family the 7 steps; parents initial the
  checklist.

### Day 2 (Tuesday) — Poisons and safe mouths (pp. 9, 13–14)

- **Morning Meeting — What Goes in My Mouth:** Build the class definition of
  **poison** from children’s answers (write every idea). Walk the *room*
  together spotting containers that *might* hold poisons (cleaning bottles,
  medicine); mark each with the agreed hazard sign (frowning face or skull and
  crossbones — or the national symbol). Rule: *hazard sign = give to an adult
  at once, never touch.*
- **Small Group — Sort, chart, solve:** (1) Sort real items + pictures into
  ✅/🚫 hoops. (2) Build a **picture graph**: two columns, children stick and
  count (*“9 safe, 7 unsafe — which column is taller?”*). (3) Solve berry/nut
  dilemmas: *“How would YOU decide if a berry is good to eat?”* Accept only
  safe answers (ask a grown-up; never taste unknowns). (4) Draw poison posters
  with hazard signs for the classroom walls; present them.
- *Centers:* Manipulative — independent re-sort + tally. Art — poison posters.
  Literacy — “safe/unsafe” word + picture match.
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play** + animal-food discussion (Planner p. 14): rabbit/
  carrot vs. cat/mouse — then the market-walk version: from the fence or on a
  walk, survey *eat* vs. *locked-away* items; back inside, list findings.
- *Story Time:* children narrate the berry story with roles (child, mother,
  berry) — safe ending only.
- *Closing:* each child: “One thing I will NEVER put in my mouth is…”
- *Home link:* family hazard hunt — list 3 locked-away items; draw the hazard
  sign together.

### Day 3 (Wednesday) — Sickness, wellness, vaccinations (pp. 7, 12)

- **Morning Meeting — Being Sick and Well:** Deep discussion: *What does sick
  feel like? What helps?* (rest, water, clean habits, nurse/doctor, medicine
  from adults only). Vaccinations prevent big diseases — small pain now beats
  big sickness later. Children tell immunization stories one by one; teacher
  scribes **every word** for parents. Invite the nurse/doctor to answer
  questions.
- **Small Group — Stories + kindness mail:** illustrate the scribed stories;
  mount a *Sick → Well* display (two sides). Make **get-well cards** for any
  absent child (draw + dictate message). Start a feelings corner in the
  hospital box: *“I feel scared when…”* cards children can draw any time.
- *Centers:* Drama — full **hospital corner** (kit, bandages, beds, waiting
  bench, “doctor” notebooks). Literacy — illness/hospital/shot books; teacher
  read-aloud + “what would you do?” pauses. Art — *sick face / well face*
  portraits.
- *Outdoor:* **Water Play.**
- *Story Time:* book about hospitals or new siblings; name feelings characters
  show.
- *Closing:* “Who helps us get well? What can WE do for a sick friend?”
- *Home link:* read the scribed story together; parents add one line.

### Day 4 (Thursday) — Exercise science + nose blowing (pp. 8, 11)

- **Morning Meeting — Exercise Is Healthy:** Feel muscles; locate heartbeat;
  predict (*“Will it beat faster after running?”*), test (run/dance 2 min),
  conclude together. Study the skeleton/muscle poster + athlete photos;
  welcome any visiting athlete/health helper with prepared questions. Set the
  class exercise challenge + ☀️ chart (a sun sticker per active day).
- **Small Group — Nose-blowing lab:** (1) Gentle-blow technique (hard blowing
  hurts ears — show the face diagram for ready children). (2) Experiments:
  whistle parade with rhythms; straw bubbles (*blow, don’t suck* — practice
  the difference first); tissue-then-bin-then-wash relay. (3) Draw *“having a
  cold”* + label one feeling word.
- *Centers:* Block — design an exercise park with labeled parts. Art — cold-day
  pictures. Manipulative — straw-blow painting (control the breath!).
- *Outdoor:* **Sand Play** + led circuit: run → jump → stretch → heartbeat
  check.
- *Story Time:* movement story — children act every verb.
- *Closing:* “What did exercise do to your heart today? Why is that good?”
- *Home link:* family 5-minute workout; child teaches nose-blow steps.

### Day 5 (Friday) — Street safety preview + week review (p. 2)

- **Morning Meeting — Safety in the Streets:** roads vs. paths; hold hands;
  stop-look-decide. Demonstrate with toy cars on a floor road; children direct
  the “traffic” correctly.
- **No small groups:** centers free choice; checkers re-verify handwashing
  steps for any child; finish Germ Book pages.
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play.**
- *Story Time:* class vote — best story + best song of the week, performed by
  children.
- *Closing:* week review + preview (teeth science next week!).
- 👀 **Look for by Friday:** 7 steps solo + teaches another child; defines
  poison in own words; sorts 8+ items and reads the graph (more/fewer);
  dictates a sequenced germ story; explains exercise → strong heart.

## WEEK 2 — My Body (teeth science + habit mastery)

**Weekly goals:** Children explain teeth, plaque and cavities; brush all tooth
surfaces in circles and say when to brush; run handwashing/nose routines
independently; use health vocabulary in sentences (*germs, plaque, cavity,
exercise, vaccine, poison, muscles*).

### Day 1 (Monday) — Handwashing mastery + peer teaching (pp. 5, 10)

- **Morning Meeting:** checkers report the week’s handwashing; two “experts”
  demo blind (narrating each step); class judges with the checklist. New
  challenge: **food washing + dish washing** demo (Planner p. 5) — *germs hide
  on mango skin and plates too.*
- **Small Group — Instruction studio:** teams produce final washing-corner
  cards (numbered pictures + dictated captions); mount at child height.
  Advanced teams add a Germ Book sequel.
- *Centers/Outdoor (Water Play)/Story/Closing:* child-led chants; KG-2
  volunteers demo for KG-1 if timetabled.
- *Home link:* photo/drawing of the home washing spot; is soap + own towel
  ready?

### Day 2 (Tuesday) — Mouth safety graphs (pp. 9, 13–14)

- **Morning Meeting — Keeping Healthy, big picture:** collect the full healthy
  list (wash, brush, good food, exercise, rest, vaccines, safe mouths).
  Classify: *body-inside habits* vs. *body-outside habits*? Let children argue
  it out.
- **Small Group — Graph day:** re-sort both sets; build the final wall graph;
  count, compare, and **present**: each team explains one column to the class.
  Solve two new dilemmas (unknown berries; shiny medicine bottle that “looks
  like candy” — *looks can fool us; ask a grown-up*).
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play**; optional market/shop walk (Planner p. 14): tally
  *eat* vs. *locked-away* items on clipboards; graph back in class.
- *Story/Closing:* role-play the “candy-looking medicine” scene — safe ending.
- *Home link:* child presents the wall-graph idea at home with bottle tops as
  counters.

### Day 3 (Wednesday) — Teeth science (Planner p. 6)

- **Morning Meeting — Brushing Teeth:** (1) *Why teeth?* chew food + protect
  health; bad teeth → sickness. (2) The **plaque story, full version**: sticky
  germ layer loves sugar/sweets/fizzy drinks, drills brown holes (**cavities**)
  that hurt; the toothbrush is plaque’s enemy. Examine sugar + wrappers; sort
  snacks into *teeth friends / teeth enemies*. (3) Egg-box mouths + sticks:
  drill **circles — front, back, both sides, top AND bottom** — while singing
  *“This is the way we brush our teeth…”*; individuals demo; class coaches.
  Rule: **brush after meals**.
- **Small Group — Handwashing (planner repeats it):** speed-with-accuracy
  round judged by checkers + one new instruction card each.
- *Centers:* Art — giant class tooth + plaque bugs. Drama — tooth clinic
  (appointments, “dentist” checks egg-box mouths). Literacy — teeth word cards
  + *friend/enemy* snack sort.
- *Outdoor:* **Sand Play** — brush giant sand teeth with big sticks in
  circles.
- *Story Time:* tooth fairy / tooth-hero story; children brush dolls’ teeth.
- *Closing:* “When do we brush? Show me the circle motion!”
- *Home link:* whole-family brush after supper; child checks parents’ circles.
  (Teacher: invite the health team where possible.)

### Day 4 (Thursday) — Exercise challenge + cold care (pp. 8, 11)

- **Morning Meeting — Exercise:** read the ☀️ chart — did we move every day?
  Try a new movement (hop, gallop, balance); predict/compare heartbeats. If an
  exercise facility trip is possible (Planner p. 14), plan it: what will we
  try? safety rules?
- **Small Group — Nose-blowing finals:** whistle rhythms by notation (long/
  short cards); straw-bubble races (gentle!); cold pictures with dictated
  captions (*“I feel… I need…”*). Restock tissue stations; assign monitors.
- *Outdoor:* **Water Play** + full exercise circuit led by child leaders.
- *Story/Closing:* movement-story performance.
- *Home link:* log one family exercise + one tissue-station check.

### Day 5 (Friday) — Street safety + Section I exhibition

- **Morning Meeting:** stop-look-hold chant + floor-road directing; name one
  rule for walkers.
- **No small groups:** curate the Section I exhibition (Germ Book, graphs,
  tooth art, get-well cards, instruction cards); children rehearse explaining
  one piece.
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play.**
- *Story Time:* vote + perform favourites.
- *Closing:* “I keep my body healthy by…” — full sentences, one per child.
- 👀 **Look for by Friday:** circles on all tooth surfaces + “after meals”;
  snacks sorted friends/enemies with reasons; checklist-accurate 7-step wash;
  graph read (counts + more/fewer); 5+ health words used correctly.

# SECTION II — CARING FOR OUR COMMUNITY (Weeks 3–4)

**Big idea (Planner p. 15):** The classroom is a community; the town is a
community. KG-2 children take real responsibility: keep rules and explain
them, read road signs, meet and thank helpers, run reuse and compost projects,
and leave every place cleaner. **Teachers model** — and KG-2 children model
for KG-1.

## Master materials — Section II

Big paper, paste/tape, crayons, markers; cardboard + red/yellow/green paper
(traffic lights); clean collected “trash” (boxes, bottles, paper, fabric),
toy cars/trucks, bikes/wagons; compost bags: plastic bags, soil, grass, fruit/
veggie scraps, water + comparison non-rotters (plastic, tops) (p. 22);
community-place pictures; brooms, cloths, basins; dress-ups; chart paper;
clipboards for walks.

## WEEK 3 — Caring for Our Community, part 1

**Weekly goals:** Children define community; keep and justify class rules;
read red/yellow/green (+ local signs) and play the traffic game by the rules;
sort along three lines (safe/unsafe, reuse/throw-away, rots/never-rots) and
chart results.

### Day 1 (Monday) — Our community + our rules (pp. 17–18, 24)

- **Morning Meeting — Rules + Knowing Our Community:** (1) Recite rules; debate
  one: *“What would happen without it?”* (2) Define **community** together
  (people + places + services). Children report visits (market, hospital…):
  *What did you see/do there? Who worked there? What did they do?* (3)
  **Advanced:** generate the full list of places in *our* community; assign
  each to the painting teams. Rules stay 3–5, positive, big, illustrated —
  review which still fit (rules can change as the class changes, p. 18).
- **Small Group — Community Group Painting:** road-grid paper at eye level;
  teams draw assigned buildings; partners draw/color; leaders label with
  teacher-scribed signs. Present: each team introduces its building.
- *Centers:* Block — zoned town (homes/market/clinic/school) + roads. Drama —
  market stalls with goods + prices in bottle tops. Literacy — community word
  wall begins.
- *Outdoor:* **Trash Walk** (supervised, teacher + parents): *safe-in-bag /
  unsafe-tell-adult* protocol; tally what we find; back inside decide:
  wrappers? bottles? (reuse bin / trash / tell-adult).
- *Story Time:* market/hospital story with helper role-play.
- *Closing:* “Our community is…? One rule that keeps it safe is…?”
- *Home link:* name-and-point walk past 2 community places; report back.

### Day 2 (Tuesday) — Why rules? + reuse workshop (pp. 18, 21, 27)

- **Morning Meeting — Reasons for rules + Reusing Materials:** (1) Each rule:
  children act the *keeping* and (safely, humorously) the *breaking* — then
  state the reason. (2) Reuse launch: teacher’s reused object + the reuse
  station; brainstorm *“what else instead of throwing away?”*; open the class
  collection box with sorting trays.
- **Small Group — Reuse workshop:** sort donations (reuse/throw-away); build
  first trash-collages + one 3-D reused object per team; start the weekly
  **trash chart** (*how much did we throw away today?* — count bags/items).
- *Centers:* Art — collage studio. Block — box-and-tube builds. Manipulative —
  sorting trays + tally sheets.
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play.**
- *Story Time:* dictated epic *“The Trash That Kept Piling Up…”* — children
  supply the rescue plan (reuse! clean-up day! more bins! letters to the
  community — Planner p. 21).
- *Closing:* “What did you rescue from the trash today?”
- *Home link:* reuse interview — what does our family reuse? Draw it.

### Day 3 (Wednesday) — Clean community crew (pp. 20, 26–27)

- **Morning Meeting — Keeping Our Community Clean:** jobs briefing: wipe,
  stack, scrape, sweep, sort. Watch/thank the trash collector if they come;
  ask *“Where does our trash go?”* + *“How can we make less of it?”* Plan
  Friday Cleaning Day (old clothes, job chart). Discuss old toys: give, trade,
  donate (which of ours could go?).
- **Small Group — Triple sort + chart:** (1) safe-to-pick-up vs. never-touch;
  (2) reuse vs. throw-away; (3) **Advanced:** rots-back-to-earth vs. stays-
  forever. Chart all three. Drill the chain: *touched trash → wash hands!*
  (germ link to Section I). Finish collages for exhibition.
- *Centers:* Practical life — real cleaning tools, folding, bead-sorting;
  Drama — street-crew with vests + toy brooms; Art — *dirty street → clean
  street* before/after pairs.
- *Outdoor:* **Trash Walk 2** — compare tallies with Monday: *cleaner? why?*
- *Story Time:* old-toy donation story; children choose one class toy to
  gift/repair.
- *Closing:* “What did the crew clean today?”
- *Home link:* child owns one home clean-up job all week; parents report.

### Day 4 (Thursday) — Road signs & signals (pp. 25–26)

- **Morning Meeting — Street Signs and Signals:** build the big traffic light
  together (red/yellow/green meanings + *“caught in the street on yellow?
  keep going steadily to the side — never run back”*). Who do signals protect?
  (walkers, bicycles, cars). Urban: rehearse the real crossing routine.
  Rural: colored-paper lights drill. Invite a traffic officer if possible.
- **Small Group — Make, play, apply:** (1) Each team builds a working light
  (flaps/sliders). (2) **Advanced game:** music + leader signals (red = freeze,
  yellow = slow/wait, green = go) — add pedestrian/other local signs as extra
  commands. (3) Apply: drive block-town cars + outdoor bikes/wagons **only**
  on green; sign-holders direct. Install lights permanently in Block corner.
- *Centers:* Block — roads, bridges, towns, vehicles. Art — signal collages +
  child-made sign cards for the collection.
- *Outdoor:* **Sorting Outside** (buckets: reuse/throw-away/rottables) + full
  traffic game with bikes.
- *Story Time:* officer visit or “safe crossing” drama with narrator.
- *Closing:* rapid-fire color quiz (children hold colors, class responds).
- *Home link:* spot one real sign/signal; rehearse the crossing routine.

### Day 5 (Friday) — Walk briefing + Cleaning Day (pp. 20, 28)

- **Morning Meeting — Community Walk briefing:** route, buddies, line order,
  safety protocol (hold hands, stop at every road, whistle = freeze), mission
  clipboards (find: 3 buildings, 2 helpers, 1 sign, 1 clean place, 1 dirty
  place).
- **No small groups — Cleaning Day:** old clothes, job chart, teams rotate
  (puzzles, beads, sand table, dolls’ bath, sweeping); toy safety inspection —
  broken/unsafe to the fixing box; parents help mend. Celebration circle in
  the sparkling room.
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play** + rehearsal walk around the school with clipboards.
- *Story Time:* clean-up song + favourite community story.
- *Closing:* “Our classroom is clean because every one of us…?”
- 👀 **Look for by Friday:** defines community (people/places/services);
  justifies 3 rules; reads 3 light colors + acts them in game; triple-sort
  with chart; names the trash chain (bag → collector → ?).

## WEEK 4 — Consolidation: Young Citizens’ Week

**Weekly goals:** Children combine health + community knowledge independently;
run the compost investigation with predictions and records; complete a real
community walk/visit with findings; present learning to parents. *(The planner
overviews Weeks 1–3; Week 4 consolidates “depending on the interest of
children.”)*

### Day 1 (Monday) — Compost scientists (pp. 22–23)

- **Morning Meeting:** recall songs/rules in child-led medley. Launch question:
  *“Which things go back into the earth — and which pile up forever?”*
- **Small Group — Compost experiment, full protocol:** (1) Predict-sort items:
  *will change / may change / will never change* (record on chart). (2) Each
  child builds a bag: ½ cup soil + spoon of grass + cup of torn fresh scraps +
  ~40 ml water; squash, seal, hang on the class line; build comparison bags
  (plastic/foam instead of scraps). (3) Draw dated predictions (4–6 weeks
  ahead). (4) Job rota: daily squishers, air-openers, smell-checkers (*smell =
  too wet/needs air*). Revisit predictions weekly; plant seeds in finished
  compost; optional recycling-center visit.
- *Centers/Outdoor:* Water Play + compost-job practice; free choice.
- *Story/Closing:* “What will your scraps look like in one month?”
- *Home link:* home scrap bowl started; child explains the experiment.

### Day 2 (Tuesday) — Helpers & clinic (bridge to January’s theme)

- **Morning Meeting — Community helpers:** profile 4–5 (nurse, doctor, teacher,
  trash collector, traffic officer, market seller): *What service? What tools?
  What happens if they stop?* Prepare thank-you questions for walk day.
- **Small Group — Box-town build:** teams construct + label clinic, market,
  home, school, post stall; connect with roads + working lights; write (scribe)
  opening signs (*“CLINIC — we help sick people”*).
- *Centers:* Drama — full clinic (reception, beds, nurse caps, notebooks,
  kind-phrase cards). Block — box town + traffic. Literacy — helper word wall
  + tool matching.
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play** + child-refereed traffic game.
- *Story/Closing:* perform “A Day at the Clinic.”
- *Home link:* thank a real helper; bring the story back.

### Day 3 (Wednesday) — Community walk / visit day (pp. 19, 28)

- **Morning (extended):** the mission walk — market / clinic / primary school /
  recycling point. En route: clipboards out, buddies tight, whistle discipline.
  At each stop: observe, ask the prepared question, thank the worker.
- **Primary-school visit protocol (Planner p. 19):** pre-briefed objects
  (pencil, ruler, book), names, behavior; debrief: uniforms? rooms? *What do
  children write with — why? What is reading? What do people read?*
- **Afternoon:** sequence the walk into the class book (draw + dictate +
  order pages); complete mission tallies; graph *clean vs. dirty spots*.
- *Closing:* “Best thing I saw / most important job I saw.”
- *Home link:* child narrates the whole walk from the class book.

### Day 4 (Thursday) — Festival of favourite stations

- **Morning Meeting:** vote the top 4 stations (e.g., germ lab, tooth clinic,
  traffic town, reuse studio, clinic corner); station leaders elected and
  briefed.
- **Work Time:** free-flow festival — leaders teach, teachers + parents
  observe against checkpoints and collect folder samples.
- *Outdoor:* **Final trash walk** — leave the street spotless; plant or tidy
  one green spot if possible.
- *Story Time:* dress rehearsal of the parent-exhibition tour.
- *Closing:* specific praise per child (one mastered skill each).
- *Home link:* “Teach me your station tonight.”

### Day 5 (Friday) — Exhibition for parents + theme close

- **Morning Meeting:** songs medley + compost-chart update (any changes yet?);
  tour guides assigned.
- **No small groups — Exhibition:** Germ Book, graphs, tooth art, box town +
  traffic demo, collages, walk book, compost line. Each child presents one
  piece to visiting parents. Collect parent pledges (*one healthy habit we’ll
  keep*).
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play.**
- *Story Time:* farewell song to December; preview January — *My Neighborhood:
  roads, water, air, markets, playgrounds, helpers!*
- *Closing:* “I keep my community healthy and safe by…” (full sentence each).
- 👀 **Look for by Friday (whole theme):** independent hygiene routines +
  peer teaching; full-sentence health/community explanations; graphs read and
  presented; traffic rules applied in play; rules justified; 3+ places + 3+
  helpers named with services; compost prediction + one observed change
  recorded; safe, disciplined walking in line.

## Appendix A — Songs, chants & games (KG-2)

1. **Handwashing chant** (children compose the final verse):
   *“Wet, soap, bubbles bright — palms and backs, left and right! Fingers,
   tips and in-between — rinse them off, now hands are clean!”*
2. **Brushing song** (Planner p. 6): *“This is the way we brush our teeth…”*
   — circles, all surfaces, after meals.
3. **Street chant:** *“Stop — Look — Listen — Hold a hand!”* (+ whistle =
   freeze on walks).
4. **Traffic game, full rules** (Week 3+): leader signals; sign-holders add
   extra stop/go commands; drivers who miss a signal “park” for one round.
5. **Sorting races:** teams sort trays against the sand timer, then verify
   each other.
6. **Clean-up song:** daily 11:00–11:15, led by the song leader.
