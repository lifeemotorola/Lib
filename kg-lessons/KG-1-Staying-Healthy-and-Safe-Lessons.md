# KG-1 Lessons — Staying Healthy and Safe (December, 4 weeks)

**Level:** Kindergarten I (KG-1, ≈ ages 4–5) — Beginner → Intermediate
**Theme:** Staying Healthy and Safe · **Month:** December
**Source:** *Liberia National ECD Curriculum: Teacher Planner, 3- to 6-Year-Olds —
Staying Healthy and Safe*, Ministry of Education / Bureau of Early Childhood
Education, 2017 (`909924202-2-Staying-Healthy.pdf`). Page numbers below
(“Planner p. N”) are the printed planner pages.

**Sections:** I. Keeping My Body Healthy (Weeks 1–2) · II. Caring for Our
Community (Weeks 3–4).

## How to teach KG-1 from these plans

- **Model everything first.** Do each step yourself while the children watch —
  washing, brushing (on the egg-box teeth), nose blowing — then let them copy
  you one small step at a time.
- **Short sittings, lots of doing.** Keep morning-meeting talk brief; put the
  learning in songs, chants, sorting games and centers.
- **Accept pointing, naming and one-word answers**, then stretch them gently
  (“Yes — soap! Soap makes … bubbles!”).
- **Repeat across days.** The planner repeats Handwashing in Weeks 1 and 2 on
  purpose — habits need repetition.
- **Assess by watching**, using the “👀 Look for” checkpoints each Friday.
  There are no written tests in KG-1.

Daily routine: Morning Meeting 9:00–9:15 · Work Time + Small Groups 9:15–11:00
· Clean-Up 11:00–11:15 · Outdoor 11:15–12:00 · Wash-Up/Lunch 12:00–12:45 ·
Rest 12:45–14:15 · Story Time 14:15–14:30 · Closing 14:30–15:00 · Parents join
in all day (volunteer, sing, learn activities for home).

# SECTION I — KEEPING MY BODY HEALTHY (Weeks 1–2)

**Big idea (Planner p. 1):** Health is being well in body and mind, getting
along with others, and eating healthy food. Children learn to keep clean
(wash hands, blow noses), move and rest their bodies, and know what is safe
to put in their mouths. **The teacher models every healthy habit** — eats the
snack, exercises with the children, washes hands with them.

## Master materials — Section I (collect once, reuse all fortnight)

Soap, plastic bowl, water, cup for pouring (no running water? see Week 1,
Day 1), towel/washcloth per child (from home, named), toothbrush + toothpaste,
comb/hairbrush, nail brush, sponge, poster or drawing of children washing
(Beginner, Planner p. 4); egg boxes + small sticks + white paint or chalk,
sweet wrappers/sugar packet (Planner p. 6); straws (pawpaw-leaf stems),
whistles, tissues, waste baskets (p. 11); food and NON-poisonous non-food items
for sorting — blocks, safety pins, buttons, small toys, coins — plus pictures
of poisonous/non-poisonous items, paper, markers, tape (pp. 9, 13); paper,
pencils, crayons; doctor kit, bandages (drama corner); poster of body/skeleton
if available; pictures of athletes (male and female).

## WEEK 1 — Keeping My Body Healthy, part 1

**Weekly goals:** Most children can wash hands with soap step by step with the
teacher; name things that keep us clean; say what healthy bodies need (clean
body, good food, exercise, rest); begin to sort “in my mouth / not in my
mouth.”

### Day 1 (Monday) — Handwashing (Planner pp. 4–5, 10)

**Morning Meeting — Keeping Clean and Healthy**
- *Objective:* Children begin to understand why we keep our bodies clean.
- *Materials:* soap, sponge, towel, toothbrush/paste, comb, bucket of water,
  poster of children washing.
- *Steps:* (1) Welcome, greetings, name the week’s topic. (2) Hold up each
  cleaning item — children name it (or repeat after you) and say what it is
  for; demonstrate anything unfamiliar; let children touch/smell the soap and
  taste a tiny bit of toothpaste. (3) Use the poster to show ways we keep
  clean. (4) Talk with questions: *What do we wash our bodies with? How do we
  wash our face? ears? hands? hair? teeth? How do we keep hair neat? noses
  clean? Why do we wash? When do we wash our hands? How do you feel when you
  are clean?*
- *Small Group — Handwashing practice:* Demonstrate the full sequence, chanting
  each step: wet hands → soap + bubbles → palms → backs → between fingers →
  all fingers → rinse → dry completely. Explain simply: *germs on dirty hands
  travel to food and people and can make us sick.* Each child practices.
  ⚠️ **No running water:** pour one cup to wet, soap, pour a second cup to
  rinse; each child uses their own named towel — never share wash water.
  Make up a short handwashing song together and sing it every time hands are
  washed this fortnight.
- *Centers:* Art — trace around your hand, draw soap bubbles; display as
  reminder cards. Drama/house corner — doll dishes + soapy water for washing
  play. Literacy — “clean” picture cards to name.
- *Outdoor:* **Water Play** — pouring, splashing, washing toy dishes.
- *Story Time:* sing the new handwashing song; finger play about bubbles.
- *Closing:* “What new thing did you learn today?” — children show clean hands.
- *Home link:* Tell parents the exact handwashing steps so they practice them
  at home (Planner p. 5).

### Day 2 (Tuesday) — Keeping Healthy + What Goes in My Mouth (pp. 4, 9, 13)

- **Morning Meeting — Keeping Clean and Healthy (repeat & stretch):** Greet,
  repeat the week’s topic. Revisit 2–3 cleaning items quickly, then ask: *When
  do we wash our hands?* (before eating, after toilet, after blowing nose).
  Praise every child who washed that morning.
- **Small Group — What Goes in My Mouth (sorting):** (1) Ask what the word
  **poison** means; write the children’s answers on a list. (2) With the small
  group, sort real safe items into two big labeled hoops/boxes: ✅ *“Things
  That Can Go in My Mouth”* (food) and 🚫 *“Things That Must Not Go in My
  Mouth”* (blocks, pins, buttons, toys, coins). (3) Repeat with pictures of
  poisonous vs. safe items. (4) Rule to chant: *“If it is not food, ask a
  grown-up first!”*
- *Centers:* Manipulative — re-sort the two boxes independently. Art — draw one
  food that goes in my mouth. Drama — “kitchen” play: which play foods are
  safe?
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play** + teacher-led moment: *“Rabbits eat carrots and we
  eat carrots too — but cats eat mice and we must NOT eat mice!”* (Planner
  p. 14). Which animal foods can people eat?
- *Story Time:* make up a tiny story — *“Kuma finds a red berry. Is it good to
  eat? How can he decide?”* — children answer; stress: poisons can make us
  very sick, always ask an adult.
- *Closing:* each child names one safe and one unsafe item.
- *Home link:* parents name 3 safe / 3 unsafe mouth items with the child.

### Day 3 (Wednesday) — Being Sick and Well (Planner pp. 7, 12)

- **Morning Meeting — Being Sick and Well:** (1) Talk simply: sometimes our
  body feels sick; rest, clean habits and the doctor/nurse help us get well.
  **Vaccinations** stop us getting very sick — the small pain is much better
  than a big sickness. (2) *“Have you ever gotten a vaccine? What do you
  remember?”* — one child at a time tells; teacher writes each story down to
  share with parents later. (3) Invite the school nurse/doctor if possible to
  say how shots keep children free from disease.
- **Small Group — Being Sick and Being Well:** children draw a picture of
  their vaccination story (or of feeling sick vs. well); display pictures with
  the dictated stories; send copies home. If a classmate is sick, the group
  makes **get-well cards**.
- *Centers:* Drama — **hospital corner**: doctor kit, bandages, dolls as
  patients; children act out visiting the doctor and talk about their fears
  (keep a hospital box all year, Planner p. 7). Literacy — books about illness,
  hospitals, shots; teacher reads one aloud.
- *Outdoor:* **Water Play**.
- *Story Time:* read/make a book about being sick: *How does it feel? What do
  we do when we feel sick?* (tell an adult, rest, drink water).
- *Closing:* “Who helps us when we are sick?” (parents, nurse, doctor).
- *Home link:* parents read the child’s dictated story together.

### Day 4 (Thursday) — Exercise Is Healthy + Nose Blowing (pp. 8, 11)

- **Morning Meeting — Exercise Is Healthy:** (1) *Muscles and hearts grow
  strong when we move* — running, walking, dancing. Children feel their arm
  muscles. (2) Show body/skeleton poster and athlete pictures (men and women).
  If a community athlete or health helper can visit, welcome them. (3) Everyone
  runs/dances in place for 2 minutes, then feels their heartbeat: *“It beats
  faster — exercise makes it strong!”*
- **Small Group — Nose Blowing:** (1) Show how to blow gently into a tissue
  (*not too hard — it can hurt our ears*); each child practices. (2) Play
  blowing games: blow whistles in turn; blow through straws into soapy water
  (teach **blow vs. suck** first!). (3) Always: tissue → bin → **wash hands**.
  Keep tissues + waste baskets available from today on; tell parents the
  routine.
- *Centers:* Art — draw “how it feels to have a cold.” Block — build an
  “exercise park.” Manipulative — straw-blowing painting (gentle blowing only).
- *Outdoor:* **Sand Play** + 5-minute teacher-led run-and-stretch: run, jump,
  stretch, feel hearts.
- *Story Time:* act out an “exercise parade” to a clapped rhythm.
- *Closing:* “What does exercise do for our body?”
- *Home link:* families dance/run together for 5 minutes; practice nose
  blowing + handwashing.

### Day 5 (Friday) — Safety in the Streets + review (Planner p. 2)

- **Morning Meeting — Safety in the Streets (first taste; full traffic-light
  work returns in Week 3):** roads are for cars, paths for people; hold a
  grown-up’s hand near roads; stop and look. Practise “Stop — Look — Hold a
  hand” as a chant with actions.
- **No small groups (planner rhythm):** free choice in centers; teacher
  observes and revisits handwashing with any child who needs it.
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play.**
- *Story Time:* favourite song of the week (vote by show of hands) + one
  story retold by the children with teacher help.
- *Closing:* week review — “Which new things did you learn?” Name next week’s
  topic (our bodies: teeth!).
- 👀 **Look for by Friday:** washes hands with soap following most steps;
  names soap, water, towel, toothbrush; sorts 4–6 items into mouth/not-mouth;
  says one thing exercise does; blows nose into tissue and bins it.

## WEEK 2 — My Body (teeth, healthy habits again)

**Weekly goals:** Children know why teeth matter and how to brush (round
circles, all sides); handwashing and sorting habits strengthen; children use
the words *germs, healthy, sick, exercise, poison*.

### Day 1 (Monday) — Handwashing again (pp. 5, 10)

- **Morning Meeting:** greet; restate topic. Quick recall game: *“Show me with
  your hands — what is the FIRST thing before eating?”* Sing the handwashing
  song; two children demonstrate while the class chants the steps.
- **Small Group — Handwashing pictures:** trace hands, draw soap bubbles;
  mount the best as instruction cards by the washing bowl. Teacher writes each
  child’s dictated sentence (“I wash my hands before I eat.”).
- *Centers / Outdoor (Water Play) / Story / Closing:* as Week 1 Day 1, with
  children leading more of the chant.
- *Home link:* parents check: can the child do all 7 steps alone?

### Day 2 (Tuesday) — What Goes in My Mouth again (pp. 9, 13)

- **Morning Meeting — Keeping Healthy:** *“What keeps our body healthy?”*
  Collect answers (wash, good food, exercise, rest, clean teeth). Add one new
  idea: medicines and cleaning liquids are NOT food — never taste them.
- **Small Group — Sorting + chart:** re-sort the two boxes; then the teacher
  starts a **picture chart** on the wall (two columns, children stick items);
  count together: *“How many safe? How many unsafe?”* Tell a berry/nut story
  for children to solve.
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play**; if near a market or shop, take the class walk from
  Planner p. 14: children point out (never touch) things to eat vs. things
  locked away.
- *Story/Closing:* retell the berry story with a safe ending (child asks
  mother).
- *Home link:* walk around the home together — which things must stay locked
  away?

### Day 3 (Wednesday) — Brushing Teeth (Planner p. 6)

- **Morning Meeting — Brushing Teeth:** (1) *“Why are good teeth important?”*
  (to chew food; bad teeth can make us sick). (2) Tell the **plaque story**
  simply: sticky germs love sweets and fizzy drinks and make little brown
  holes (**cavities**) that hurt; plaque HATES the toothbrush! Show sweet
  wrappers and sugar. (3) Give each pair an egg-box “mouth” (white teeth, red
  gums) + stick brush; model **round circular brushing** — front, back, both
  sides, top and bottom — while everyone sings *“This is the way we brush our
  teeth … early in the morning.”* Talk: brush **after meals**.
- **Small Group — Handwashing (planner repeats it):** quick practice round +
  new children’s instruction drawings for the wall.
- *Centers:* Art — paint “sugar bugs” (germ pictures). Drama — “tooth clinic”
  with egg-box mouths. Literacy — “teeth” word card + smile pictures.
- *Outdoor:* **Sand Play** — hide (clean) sweet wrappers? No — keep food out
  of sand; instead draw giant teeth in the sand with sticks.
- *Story Time:* sing the brushing song; children brush toy animals’ teeth.
- *Closing:* “When do we brush our teeth?”
- *Home link:* brush together after supper; parents praise round-circle
  brushing. (Teacher: invite the health team if available, Planner p. 6.)

### Day 4 (Thursday) — Exercise + Nose Blowing again (pp. 8, 11)

- **Morning Meeting — Exercise:** recall: muscles, heartbeat. Try three
  movements (run, jump, stretch); feel hearts before/after. Set a class
  challenge: *“Can we exercise every day this week?”* Make a simple ☀️ chart.
- **Small Group — Nose Blowing games:** whistle parade; straw bubbles; tissue
  practice; draw “how it feels to have a cold.” Older-ready KG-1s can hear how
  nose and ears connect (keep it one sentence).
- *Outdoor:* **Water Play** + exercise parade.
- *Story/Closing:* week’s favourite movement game once more.
- *Home link:* 5-minute family exercise; tissues within the child’s reach.

### Day 5 (Friday) — Safety in the Streets + Section I round-up

- **Morning Meeting:** “Stop — Look — Hold a hand” chant; name one street rule.
- **No small groups:** centers free choice; finish and display Section I work
  (hand cards, germ pictures, tooth paintings, get-well cards).
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play.**
- *Story Time:* children vote for one story and one song to repeat.
- *Closing:* celebrate: each child says one way “I keep my body healthy.”
- 👀 **Look for by Friday:** brushes egg-box teeth in circles on all sides;
  says why teeth matter (chew food); recalls the 7 handwashing steps with the
  chant; sorts 6+ items correctly; names an adult to tell when feeling sick.

# SECTION II — CARING FOR OUR COMMUNITY (Weeks 3–4)

**Big idea (Planner p. 15):** The classroom is a community and the town is a
community. Everyone — even small children — helps keep it safe, clean and
kind: we follow rules, learn road signs, meet helpers, reuse things, and care
for the earth. **The teacher models it all**: picks up trash, reuses
materials, turns off taps/lights.

## Master materials — Section II

Big paper sheets, paste/tape, crayons; cardboard + red/yellow/green paper,
markers (traffic lights); collected clean “trash” for sorting and collages
(boxes, bottles, paper, leaves); plastic bags, soil, grass clippings, fruit/
vegetable scraps, water, plus non-rotting items (plastic, bottle tops) for the
compost experiment (Planner p. 22); pictures of community places (market,
hospital, school, church/mosque); toy cars/trucks, bikes/wagons; dress-up
clothes for helpers; brooms, cloths, water for Cleaning Day.

---

## WEEK 3 — Caring for Our Community, part 1

**Weekly goals:** Children can say what a community is (“the people and places
where we live”); follow 3–5 class rules and say why; name red = stop, green =
go, yellow = wait; sort “pick up / don’t pick up” and “reuse / throw away.”

### Day 1 (Monday) — Knowing Our Community + rules (pp. 17–18, 24)

- **Morning Meeting — Classroom Rules + Knowing Our Community:** (1) Welcome,
  greetings, rules song/recite. (2) *“What is a community?”* — our homes plus
  the places and people around us: school, market, hospital, church/mosque.
  Children recall visits (*“What did you see? Who worked there? What did they
  do?”*). Show pictures. (3) Start 3–5 class rules **in positive words**
  (“We use indoor voices”) — big letters + child illustrations.
- **Small Group — Community Group Painting:** long paper on the wall at eye
  level with crossing roads drawn; each child draws/colors one community
  building from the brainstormed list. Work in pairs — one draws, one colors.
- *Centers:* Block — build the community with roads; add cars. Drama — “market”
  corner opens. Literacy — community picture cards to name.
- *Outdoor:* **Trash Walk** (strictly supervised): teacher + parent helpers;
  children point at litter; ONLY the teacher picks up safe items; chant *“Safe
  things in the bag — unsafe things we tell a grown-up!”* Back inside: *“What
  should happen to wrappers and bottles?”*
- *Story Time:* story about a market or hospital visit; children act the
  helpers.
- *Closing:* “What is our community?” + one rule we kept today.
- *Home link:* parents walk the child past 2 community places, naming them.

### Day 2 (Tuesday) — Reasons for rules + Reusing (pp. 18, 21, 27)

- **Morning Meeting — Reasons for Classroom Rules + Reusing Materials:**
  (1) *“Why do we have rules? How do they keep us safe?”* Review the wall
  rules; children show (act out) each one. (2) *Reusing:* show one reused item
  (e.g., box → storage); *“What else can we reuse instead of throwing away?”*
  Start a class **reuse collection box**.
- **Small Group — Reusing Materials:** sort collected materials: *reuse* vs.
  *throw away*; children make a first **collage from clean trash**; count and
  chart how many things were reused.
- *Centers:* Art — trash collages continue. Manipulative — reuse sorting tray.
  Block — build with boxes and tubes.
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play.**
- *Story Time:* children’s dictated story: *“What happens if trash keeps
  piling up and up?”* (Planner p. 21) — draw the pile growing.
- *Closing:* show collages; “What did you reuse today?”
- *Home link:* find one thing at home that gets reused (rags → rugs?); parents
  tell how.

### Day 3 (Wednesday) — Keeping Our Community Clean (pp. 20, 26–27)

- **Morning Meeting — Keeping Our Community Clean:** cleaning our classroom is
  **everyone’s job**: wipe tables, put dishes away, scrape plates, tidy art
  things. If the trash collector comes, watch together and thank them. Plan
  Friday’s mini **Cleaning Day** (old clothes!).
- **Small Group — Sorting Game + collages:** sort home/classroom items into
  *“Safe to pick up on the street”* vs. *“Not safe — tell a grown-up”*; then
  *“Can be reused”* vs. *“Throw away.”* Reinforce: after touching trash →
  **wash hands!** Finish collages.
- *Centers:* Practical-life table — folding cloths, sorting beads; Drama —
  “street cleaners” with toy brooms; Art — clean vs. dirty street pictures.
- *Outdoor:* **Trash Walk** (supervised, as Monday) — compare: *“Is our street
  cleaner than Monday?”*
- *Story Time:* song about cleaning up; story of old toys given to children
  who need them (Planner p. 20).
- *Closing:* “What did you clean today?”
- *Home link:* child gets one clean-up job at home (shoes in place, plates
  carried).

### Day 4 (Thursday) — Street Signs and Signals (pp. 25–26)

- **Morning Meeting — Signs:** show the class traffic light: 🔴 **red = STOP**,
  🟢 **green = GO**, 🟡 **yellow = WAIT**. *Traffic lights keep everyone safe
  — people walking, bicycles, cars.* Urban classes: recall a real walk and how
  to cross; rural classes: practice with colored paper “lights.”
- **Small Group — Make + play traffic lights:** each group makes a cardboard
  light (3 colored circles). Play the freeze game: music plays, leader holds
  up a color — red: FREEZE; green: march; yellow: slow/wait. Keep the lights
  in the Block corner all year for road play.
- *Centers:* Block — roads, bridges, towns + cars; Outdoor bikes/wagons follow
  sign-holders. Art — traffic-light color collages.
- *Outdoor:* **Sorting Outside** — sort playground items (leaves, stones, paper
  scraps) into reuse/throw-away buckets + traffic-light freeze game outside.
- *Story Time:* invite a traffic officer / community helper if possible; else
  act out “crossing the street safely.”
- *Closing:* hold up colors — class shouts the meaning.
- *Home link:* parents point out one sign or signal on the way home; practice
  stop/go/wait.

### Day 5 (Friday) — Community Walk talk + Cleaning celebration (pp. 20, 28)

- **Morning Meeting — Talk about the Community Walk:** where we will go, what
  we will look for, how we stay safe (line, hand-holding, stop at roads).
  Rehearse the safety chant.
- **No small groups:** **mini Cleaning Day** in old clothes — each child has a
  job (beads sorted, puzzles checked, dolls bathed, sand table washed, floor
  swept). End with a celebration of the clean room. Inspect toys together:
  broken/unsafe ones go to the fixing box (invite parents to help mend).
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play** + short practice walk around the school.
- *Story Time:* favourite community story; sing the clean-up song.
- *Closing:* “Our classroom community is clean because … (we all helped!).”
- 👀 **Look for by Friday:** says what a community is (people + places);
  follows 3 rules and tells why (safety/kindness); names red/yellow/green
  meanings; sorts safe/unsafe and reuse/throw-away with 1–2 prompts.

## WEEK 4 — Consolidation: Our Healthy Community

**Weekly goals:** Children use Section I + II ideas together (clean body, clean
community); revisit favourite activities with more independence; take part in a
real community walk / visit; every child is observed against the theme
checkpoints. *(The planner gives overviews for Weeks 1–3; Week 4 consolidates
“depending on the interest of children.”)*

### Day 1 (Monday) — Healthy bodies, healthy classroom

- **Morning Meeting:** sing the handwashing + brushing songs from memory;
  children lead the steps. Recall class rules and why they matter.
- **Small Group — Compost experiment begins (Planner pp. 22–23, simplified):**
  teacher-led: sort items into *will change / may change / will never change*;
  each child fills a small bag with soil + grass + torn fruit/veggie scraps +
  a spoon of water, seals and squashes gently; hang bags on a line. Make a
  second bag with plastic bits for comparison. Predict: *“What will happen?”*
  Draw predictions. (Squeeze daily; open every other day; plant a seed in the
  finished soil later.)
- *Centers / Outdoor:* Water Play + free choice; squeeze compost bags daily.
- *Story/Closing:* “What goes back into the earth?”
- *Home link:* start a home scrap bowl for the class compost.

### Day 2 (Tuesday) — Community helpers + clinic corner

- **Morning Meeting — Community helpers first look (full unit returns in the
  My Neighborhood theme):** name 3 helpers (nurse, teacher, trash collector):
  *“What do they do for us? How do we thank them?”*
- **Small Group — Reuse build:** teams build one community building from boxes
  (clinic, market stall, home); label with teacher-written signs children
  decorate.
- *Centers:* Drama — **clinic corner**: nurse caps, bandages, dolls; practice
  kind words (*“You will feel better soon.”*). Block — hospital + roads +
  traffic lights together.
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play** + traffic-light game led by a child leader.
- *Story/Closing:* act out “a visit to the clinic.”
- *Home link:* thank a helper (greet the trash collector, thank the nurse).

### Day 3 (Wednesday) — Community walk / visit day (pp. 19, 28)

- **Morning (extended):** the prepared **Community Walk** — market, clinic,
  primary school, or recycling point (whichever is near and safe). Before:
  safety rules + what to look for (buildings, helpers, signs, clean/dirty
  places). During: stop, look, ask workers one question (*“Where does our
  trash go?”*). After: draw the walk.
- **If visiting the primary school (Planner p. 19):** day-before prep with
  real objects (pencil, ruler, exercise book); learn helpers’ names and
  expected behavior; after the visit discuss: uniforms? rooms? what do
  children write with — and why? what is reading?
- *Afternoon:* draw + dictate walk stories; display in order as a class book.
- *Closing:* “What did you see? What was your favourite part?”
- *Home link:* child retells the walk to the family.

### Day 4 (Thursday) — Favourite-activities festival

- **Morning Meeting:** vote for favourite activities to repeat (handwashing
  chant, traffic game, collages, hospital corner…).
- **Work Time:** run the top 3 as free-flow stations; teacher + parent
  volunteers support; observe and note each child against checkpoints.
- *Outdoor:* **Trash Walk** finale + free play — *“Our street is clean because
  we care!”*
- *Story Time:* children perform one story for another class if possible.
- *Closing:* congratulate specific efforts (“Awa remembered all 7
  handwashing steps!”).
- *Home link:* parents ask: “What was your favourite December activity?”

### Day 5 (Friday) — Celebration + theme close

- **Morning Meeting:** “Stop — Look — Hold a hand” + handwashing song medley;
  finish compost predictions chart (what changed so far?).
- **No small groups:** exhibition of Section II work for parents (paintings,
  collages, traffic lights, walk book). Children guide parents and explain one
  piece each.
- *Outdoor:* **Free Play.**
- *Story Time:* goodbye song to the theme; preview January (My Neighborhood:
  roads, markets, helpers!).
- *Closing:* each child: “I keep my community clean and safe by ….”
- 👀 **Look for by Friday (whole theme):** completes handwashing + nose-blow
  routines; explains red/yellow/green; sorts safe/unsafe + reuse/throw-away;
  follows class rules and states one reason; names 3 community places and 2
  helpers; describes one compost change; walks safely in line holding hands.

## Appendix A — Songs, chants & games (KG-1)

1. **Handwashing chant** (make up your own tune; sing at every wash):
   *“Wet, soap, bubbles bright — palms and backs, oh what a sight! Between the
   fingers, fingers too — rinse them clean, now we’re through!”*
2. **Brushing song** (Planner p. 6): *“This is the way we brush our teeth,
   brush our teeth, brush our teeth … early in the morning.”* (round circles!)
3. **Street chant:** *“Stop — Look — Hold a hand! Stop — Look — Hold a hand!”*
   (stamp, shade eyes, clasp hands).
4. **Traffic-light freeze game** (Week 3+): music + leader shows colors.
5. **Clean-up song:** any tidy-up tune, sung daily 11:00–11:15.
