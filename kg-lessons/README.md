## Level mapping

The national planners are written for mixed ages 3–6 and differentiate every
activity as **Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced**. The mapping used here is:

- **KG-1 (≈ ages 4–5)** → Beginner activities, growing into Intermediate:
  the teacher models each step, children watch, imitate, point, name and sort
  with help, draw and dictate, and learn through songs and chants.
- **KG-2 (≈ ages 5–6)** → Intermediate activities, growing into Advanced:
  children explain ideas in their own words, make charts and graphs,
  write or dictate stories, make signs and labels, and play rule-based games.

Adjust to your class: a strong KG-1 group can try KG-2 extensions, and a young
KG-2 group can revisit KG-1 modeling steps. The planners themselves say each
section may last one or two weeks “depending on the interest of children.”

## Daily routine (all ECD days, from the planners)

| Time | Block |
|---|---|
| 9:00 – 9:15 | Morning Meeting (welcome, greetings, topic of the week) |
| 9:15 – 11:00 | Work Time — Learning Centers (child’s choice: Art, Block, Drama, Literacy, Manipulative) + 1–2 teacher-led Small Groups |
| 11:00 – 11:15 | Clean-Up |
| 11:15 – 12:00 | Outdoor Time |
| 12:00 – 12:45 | Wash-Up, Lunch |
| 12:45 – 14:15 | Rest & Quiet Time (children who can’t sleep may “read” books) |
| 14:15 – 14:30 | Group Story Time (games, stories, finger plays, songs, dramatization) |
| 14:30 – 15:00 | Closing Meeting (review of the day, sharing, planning tomorrow) |
| All day | Parents: volunteer in small groups, join songs/games/stories, learn an activity to practice at home |

## Assessment

ECD assessment is **observation, not written tests**. Each week in the lesson
files ends with “Look for” checkpoints — things most children should be able
to do, say, or show by Friday. Watch children during centers, small groups and
outdoor time; note progress; adjust the next week. Never keep a child from
play to “finish work.”

## Using these lessons with the course-pack app

The same content is wired into the generator as the **Kindergarten (ECD)**
subject: choose *National Curriculum → Lesson plan → Kindergarten (ECD) →
KG-I / KG-II*, tick the Staying Healthy and Safe units, set **2 weeks per
unit** (the planner’s rhythm), and generate Daily or Weekly plans to print or
export to Word. Course packs for KG remain cover pages only, as before.

## Notes on the source

- Planner page numbers cited in the lessons (e.g. “Planner p. 5”) are the
  printed page numbers inside *Staying Healthy and Safe*, not the PDF pages.
- The planner gives weekly overviews for December Weeks 1–3 only; Week 4 here
  is a **consolidation week** (revision, favourite activities repeated,
  community walk, observation checklists), consistent with the planner’s
  “one or two weeks depending on the interest of children.”
- In the planner’s “What Goes in My Mouth” small-group activity (p. 13), the
  Advanced column is misprinted (it repeats finger-painting steps). The KG-2
  lessons use a draw-and-label + chart extension instead, matching the
  activity’s objective.
