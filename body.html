Usage & privacy

Nothing is uploaded. The platform has no analytics and makes no network request of its own. If you switch counting on, it keeps a small tally in this browser's storage only — how many packs, plans, Word files and print runs this device produced. It is useful for a school that must report to a principal, a sponsor or a ministry, and it is the only evidence a buyer or donor will ever see that the platform is used. The tally leaves the device only when you click Export usage report, which downloads a file you decide what to do with.

Counting is off.

Turning counting off, or clearing this browser's site data, deletes the tally. Nothing else on the platform changes: course packs, printing, the teaching library and the AI tutor work the same either way.
